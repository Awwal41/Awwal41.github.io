About

Before Michigan I trained as a chemical and petroleum engineer at the University of Lagos. That work is still in print: liquid loading in gas wells, sand production, gas-hydrate temperature, biodiesel catalysis, and later studies of enhanced oil recovery and carbon storage.

The same habit of building shows up in software. Public projects include simulation agents and research codes. Private ones include a document assistant and tools for running models on a laptop, a cluster, or a phone. Those are described here at the level of the contribution. The internals stay in the repository they belong to.

Selected work

Research first, then the products and tools. Private items are labeled, and the repository stays closed.

2026 Research

TurboChIMES

First-author preprint on multi-layered machine-learned interatomic models. The models are built so atomistic simulations stay trustworthy and become fast enough to use in materials design. This is the work I presented, in three minutes, when the audience voted me People’s Choice at Michigan’s Three Minute Thesis competition.

Preprint

2026 Research

Hierarchical transfer learning

Second author, with Rebecca Lindsey and colleagues at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, in npj Computational Materials. The paper is a strategy for carrying an interatomic model into a new chemistry, so the next system starts from what the model already knows.

Paper

2026 Research

Alchemical compression

Current work with T. Sundberg and Rebecca Lindsey on shrinking the combinatorial explosion of elements inside a machine-learned interatomic potential. It is on the public record as a 2026 AIChE contribution.

2025 Software

MaterialSim

A public agent for computational materials work. You describe a molecular-dynamics run in a sentence. It sets the simulation up, computes properties such as structure and transport, and talks through what came back. The core engine runs locally.

Source

2026 Software Private

EZRA

Easy Zero-setup Routing Assistant, a researcher-facing toolkit for open language models. It downloads a model, runs a personal agent on the machine in front of you, turns documents into text a model can read, and stages the same job on a SLURM cluster. I built it for materials and chemistry work.

2025 Research

Droplet-film model for liquid loading

A physics-informed machine-learning model of the droplet film that forms when a gas well can no longer lift its liquid. The documentation is public. It continues the liquid-loading problem I first published on with Kingsley Abhulimen.

Documentation 2022 paper

2025 Research

Sand production

First-author study of how to predict and manage sand production in oil and gas wells from field data, published in Petroleum Science and Engineering. Public notebooks on reservoir rock typing are the earlier layer of the same problem.

Paper Rock typing

2025 Product Private

Edna

A document assistant with a desktop app and a web client on top of a retrieval system. You choose the folders, ask a question, and get an answer that points back at the passage. It can live on one computer or run as a service you deploy yourself.

2023– Software

ChIMES toolchain

Day-to-day research engineering in the public ChIMES stack used for machine-learned interatomic models: an active-learning driver, least-squares fitting, and calculators that connect the model to outside simulation codes.
